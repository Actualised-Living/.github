How to use it

Drag to turn the figure, scroll or pinch to zoom, Shift-drag to slide it. Tap anywhere on the body to drop a pain point, then set how strong it is and what it feels like. Tap a point again to edit it. Arrow keys turn the figure, + and − zoom.
